Claude Haiku 4.5:
The blur you're experiencing when the player moves in your top-down shooter is likely a **motion blur effect** caused by the camera's frame rate not syncing properly with the physics update rate, or it could be caused by an actual motion blur post-processing effect. Here are the main solutions:

## Fix the Physics-Camera Sync Issue

This is the most common cause. **Make sure your Fixed Timestep matches your target frame rate:**

1. Go to **Edit > Project Settings > Time**
2. Set **Fixed Timestep** to **0.02** (for 50 physics updates per second, which works well at 60 FPS)
3. Alternatively, set it to **0.0167** for roughly 60 physics updates per second

**Why this matters:** If your physics (where the player moves) updates at a different rate than your camera follows, the camera will lag slightly behind, creating blur as it "catches up."

---

## Adjust the Cinemachine Camera Settings

Your Cinemachine component has smoothing settings that might be too aggressive:

1. **In the Cinemachine Virtual Camera component**, find the **Body** section
2. Reduce the **Damping** values (especially **X Damping** and **Y Damping**) to lower values like **0.1** instead of the default
3. Alternatively, change the Body type from "Framing Transposer" to **"Hard Lock"** if you want zero lag (the camera will snap directly to the player with no smoothing)

If you want smoothing but less blur, try values around **0.05–0.15** for damping instead of higher values.

---

## Check for Motion Blur Post-Processing

1. Look in your scene for a **Post-process Volume** GameObject
2. If you have motion blur enabled in the post-processing settings, **disable it** or reduce its intensity

---

## Move Player in LateUpdate Instead of Update

If your player movement code is in `Update()`, try moving it to `LateUpdate()` instead. This ensures the camera updates after the player position changes, reducing perceived lag.

**Which solution should you try first?** Start with the **Fixed Timestep** setting (most likely culprit), then adjust Cinemachine damping if that doesn't fully resolve it. Let me know if you're still seeing blur after these changes!
